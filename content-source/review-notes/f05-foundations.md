Scope so far: the 46 modern basic and 25 voiced/semi-voiced hiragana signs, each with one sign-recognition and one kana-spelling exercise. Contracted, katakana and vocabulary items are authored in later steps. Historical kana, phonetic allophones and audio are excluded. This record is agent review, not human or native-speaker certification.

Correction reviewed on 2026-10-02 by an AI agent: the five `*-reading.json` sets now prompt from a **Latin-letter syllable cue** and require recall of the hiragana spelling in kana. The earlier displayed-sign-to-identical-sign exercises were copy tasks, not meaningful typed practice. Each row below maps its target to its exercise and review IDs; its stated Latin correspondence is the cue, not an accepted answer. Stimuli use a Latin surface/reading with a translation explaining the cue (no isolated-kana gloss, ruby, or answer-bearing Japanese surface). This metalinguistic cue is intentionally not a Japanese reading passage; the accepted answer is still Japanese kana. For は and へ the cue is the *basic written* ha/he sign rather than the particle pronunciation wa/e; を uses the conventional written wo cue with explicit object-particle context, not an underspecified /o/ cue. These spelling-context decisions apply to the corresponding reading rows and preserve distinct わ/え/お targets. Only the authored kana answer is accepted. No third-party text or audio was introduced; the original-material rights decision below still applies.

The existing `practice-kana-a-i` and `lesson-confirm-meeting-time` retain their F01 item reviews in `f01-seed.md`; only their shared contentVersion changes to 2, and the optional seed title/description now identifies its romaji objective. No seed exercise or review ID or answer objective changes.
