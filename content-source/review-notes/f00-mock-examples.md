### Choices, review prompts and mirrors

- `.mockups/flows/workplace-lesson/03-understanding.html#understanding-correct` / `#understanding-incorrect` and M-05 explain the same turn-3 intent: paraphrase and invite confirmation, not certainty or a speed request. “Faster” is not implied by 非同期; feedback is textual and remains visible. This is a contextual multiple-choice answer, **not** a grade of free Japanese production.
- `.mockups/flows/workplace-lesson/04-guided-practice.html#guided-choice`, `#guided-incorrect`, `#guided-persistent-feedback` and M-06 contrast 「もう一度説明しました」 (“I explained it again,” past statement; ruby 一度=いちど, 説明=せつめい) with 「早く説明してください」 (“Explain it quickly,” direct and abrupt in this colleague scenario; ruby 早=はや, 説明=せつめい). Neither asks for a polite repeat. The positive choice is turn 1. “Abrupt” is a contextual register judgment, not a rule that `ください` is always impolite. Reveal/skip do not constitute a correct attempt.
- `.mockups/flows/workplace-lesson/05-role-play.html#role-play-hint`, `#role-play-example` and M-07 now ask for a polite repeat before the paraphrase, and use 「結果はあとで確認できるということですね。」 (“So we can check the result later, right?”; ruby 結果=けっか, 確認=かくにん) as an **alternative**, not the literal dialogue third turn or a unique answer. M-08 extends the same thought with 「つまり、この処理は非同期で実行され、結果はあとで確認できるということですね。」 and a matching English translation. Free response is self-assessed, not exact-match graded.
- `.mockups/flows/learning-hub/03-review.html#phrase-1` / `#review-revealed`, `#phrase-2`, `#phrase-3` and M-09: phrase 1 repeats the polite request; phrase 2 「この理解で合っていますか。」 (“Is my understanding correct?”; ruby 理解=りかい, 合=あ) is a *separate* way to check understanding, not the literal third turn; phrase 3 「会議の後で対応すればよいですか。」 (“Should I handle it after the meeting?”; ruby 会議=かいぎ, 後=あと, 対応=たいおう) is an additional workplace recall item, not a claim that a meeting occurred in the clarification dialogue. The three script prompts/translations match their templates; the rated-hard example now says “check your understanding,” not “confirm the instruction.” Again/Hard/Good are self-reports, not language scores or schedules.
- `.mockups/screens/f00-topics/option-long-ja.html#mirror-m-02` labels the clarification goal 「非同期処理についての説明をもう一度お願いし、理解を確認する」 (request another explanation of asynchronous processing and check understanding). Its ruby 非同期処理=ひどうきしょり, 説明=せつめい, 一度=いちど, 願=ねが, 理解=りかい, 確認=かくにん are consistent with the connected stage. The unrelated illustrative introduction heading uses 自己紹介=じこしょうかい, 経験=けいけん, 伝=つた; it is a topic label, not an authored second lesson.
