Two typography directions

Draft choices, not an approved design system. Both themes are shown together. System fonts only; no remote assets or persistence.

Compare identical Japanese copy in two reading rhythms. The neutral light/dark canvases below are comparison scaffolding, not a fourth palette or a selected direction. Actual system font availability varies by OS; fallbacks are intentional.

Sans-led / compact

T01 — Conversation UI

Fast scanning of stages and controls; a restrained scale keeps the dialogue in the foreground.

Body/headings: system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Hiragino Sans", "Yu Gothic", Meiryo, sans-serif
UI labels: system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Hiragino Sans", "Yu Gothic", Meiryo, sans-serif
Technical identifiers: ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Consolas, "Hiragino Sans", "Yu Gothic", monospace
Proposed weights: regular 400, medium 500, semibold 600, bold 700 (system faces may synthesize unavailable weights). Tight 1.25; base 1.85; loose 2.1. No added Japanese tracking.
