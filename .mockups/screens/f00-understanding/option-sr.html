Stage 3 of 6 · why does the engineer say this?

The colleague said the process runs asynchronously and its result can be checked later. The engineer responds:

つまり、この処理しょりは非同期ひどうきで実行じっこうされるということですね。

So, this means the process runs asynchronously, right?

Choice · read-only example

  1. Restating the interpretation and inviting the colleague to confirm it.
  2. Declaring that the engineer knows the explanation is certainly correct.
  3. Asking the colleague to make the process run faster.

To make a page-local selection, use connected Understanding.

Correct · checking an interpretation

The engineer paraphrases what was heard and invites confirmation. つまり introduces the restatement; ということですね checks whether it matches the colleague’s explanation. The colleague replies はい、そのとおりです。 (“Yes, that's right.”)

Incorrect · explanation remains visible

“Certainly correct” assumes confirmation before the colleague answers. “Make the process faster” confuses timing with speed. The intended meaning is “So, this means the process runs asynchronously, right?” The colleague then confirms it. Feedback does not disappear on a timer.
