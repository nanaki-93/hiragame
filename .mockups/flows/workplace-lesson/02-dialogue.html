Four-turn text transcript

1 · Engineer · asks for repetition

すみません、もう一度いちど説明せつめいしていただけますか。

Excuse me, could you explain that once more?

Sumimasen, mō ichido setsumei shite itadakemasu ka.

2 · Colleague · explains asynchronous execution and later result check

この処理しょりは非同期ひどうきで実行じっこうされます。結果けっかはあとで確認かくにんできます。

This process runs asynchronously. You can check the result later.

Kono shori wa hidōki de jikkō saremasu. Kekka wa ato de kakunin dekimasu.

3 · Engineer · checks the interpretation

つまり、この処理しょりは非同期ひどうきで実行じっこうされるということですね。

So, this means the process runs asynchronously, right?

Tsumari, kono shori wa hidōki de jikkō sareru to iu koto desu ne.

4 · Colleague · confirms

はい、そのとおりです。

Yes, that's right.

Hai, sono tōri desu.

More to inspect in the read-only lesson preview

Illustrative phrase: もう一度いちど説明せつめいしていただけますか is a polite request to repeat an explanation; context and register matter. 非同期ひどうき means asynchronous in this engineering conversation.

Grammar note: ということですね checks a paraphrase rather than declaring it correct. Guided exercises will include intent questions and constrained response practice in F07; this preview shows their descriptions, not answer controls.

Role-play objective: politely ask for clarification, then confirm what you understood. Example response: つまり、結果けっかはあとで確認かくにんできるということですね。 This is one possible response, not the only valid answer or a graded attempt. Aids can be hidden for independent reading; no content is locked.
