Hint · paraphrase, then check

First ask again with もう一度説明していただけますか (“could you explain once more?”). Then begin your paraphrase with つまり (“so / in other words”), say what you think happens to the process, then end with ということですね to invite confirmation. You can also ask a polite follow-up. This hint is optional; it does not judge your draft.
