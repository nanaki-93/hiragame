Illustrated clarification lesson · six stages

  1. 1 · Situation — stage entry, null item checkpoint

    Meet the speakers and the goal. Open Situation

  2. 2 · Dialogue — authored turns, turn-ID checkpoints

    Read the four-turn clarification exchange with independent supports and text-only audio alternatives. Open Dialogue

  3. 3 · Understanding — choice exercises

    Answer authored choices in order; feedback stays until an explicit action. Open Understanding

  4. 4 · Guided Practice — reading and completion exercises

    Read or complete an authored prompt; check once, retry, reveal, skip or leave. The completion fixture is illustrative, not a canonical item. Open Guided Practice · See valid resume · Revisit Understanding.

  5. 5 · Role-play — production or objective-only reflection

    Compose a page-local response, then explicitly self-assess without automatic grading. If no production exercise exists, use the authored objective for self-reflection with a null item checkpoint. Open Role-play · Revisit Guided Practice.

  6. 6 · Summary — stage entry, null item checkpoint

    See current-session outcomes only, then explicitly Finish or return to Topics. Open unfinished Summary · Revisit Role-play.
